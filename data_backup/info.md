# Dura-Europos Field Photographs 

[Dura-Europos](http://https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dura-Europos) was a Hellenistic, Parthian, and Roman city in the years 300 BC and 257 CE. It was located near what is now the village of Salhiyé in Syria. In the 1930's Yale representatives excavated the site, and brought back thousands of artifacts and accompanying documentation from the site. The excavation archive is now located in New Haven.
